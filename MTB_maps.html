The map currently available on the usda.gov website is a letter size pdf document which highlights the trails in a single color that does not match the trail markers.​
This is a Jake and Bull Mountain map highlighted by a local bike shop: Fresh Bike Service. This map shows a nice route to follow when riding Jake Mt. The benefit of this route is that it takes you on fire roads for some of the climbs, then leads you to the trail to ride in the downhill direction. This base for this map is the single color usda.gov map mentioned above.

​The Jake Mt parking is a nicer lot, but it gets full and riders may encounter more horses. The Bull Mt parking is usually not as full, but the road up is a tougher drive. An AWD, 4WD, or a vehicle with more ground clearance is recommend.

Turkey Creek Sanctuary, Malabar Scrub, Jordan Scrub & Grapefruit Trails. Palm Bay, FL

There really is no good map for the entire Palm Bay mountain bike trail system that includes Turkey Creek Sanctuary, Malabar Scrub, Jordan Scrub, and the Grapefruit Trails. These 4 systems connect, but riding all 4 is local knowledge unless you can find someone's Strava map of the whole ride. This map was pulled from Strava and shows an approximately 24 mile ride. It involves wading across a canal.
The Grapefruit Trails or Fruit Trails are to the West. They are advanced, short, fun, and filled with features, obstacles, and jumps. Despite being technical, there is a bypass trail that runs the 1+ mi of trail. This trail is heavily groomed by the Brevard Mountain Bike Association (BMBA). Features and jumps change often, so a ride through is recommended.
